Project 2 — Banking Loan Application & Credit Risk Analytics

Overview

A simulated Business Analyst case study focused on improving visibility into the retail loan application lifecycle.

Business Problem

The bank needs standardized reporting for application volumes, approval/rejection rates, turnaround time, loan types, customer segments and process bottlenecks.

Objectives

Standardize business and KPI definitions.

Analyze loan application performance.

Identify process bottlenecks.

Define management reporting requirements.

Build traceable requirements and UAT coverage.

Portfolio Disclosure

This is a simulated/illustrative case study using synthetic data. Present it as a demonstration of Business Analyst skills, not as actual banking production experience.
